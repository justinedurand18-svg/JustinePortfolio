Supplier Performance Dashboard

Power BI · Data Analytics · KPI Development · Performance Management

Overview

Development and continuous improvement of a Power BI dashboard designed to monitor supplier performance and support data-driven decision-making.
The project focuses on transforming quality and delivery information into clear, actionable insights that help teams identify trends, assess performance, and prioritize improvement opportunities.

Challenge

Supplier performance monitoring involves information from different sources, making it challenging to maintain consistent indicators and obtain a comprehensive view of supplier performance.
The objective was to improve data visibility, reliability, and accessibility while providing teams with a practical tool to support performance monitoring and continuous improvement.

Approach

  • Data Consolidation: Organized and structured information from different sources to create a consistent reporting environment.
  • KPI Development: Developed and refined performance indicators related to supplier quality and delivery.
  • Dashboard Design: Created interactive Power BI visualizations to facilitate performance analysis and identify trends.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Worked with different departments to understand reporting needs and ensure the dashboard supported operational decision-making.
  • Continuous Improvement: Updated reporting tools and analytical views based on user feedback and evolving business requirements.

Outcome

The dashboard supports a more structured approach to supplier performance monitoring by making relevant indicators easier to access, interpret, and discuss.
It also contributes to cross-functional collaboration by providing a shared analytical foundation for identifying improvement priorities and supporting follow-up actions.

Skills Demonstrated

Power BI Data Visualization Data Modeling KPI Development Data Quality Stakeholder Collaboration Continuous Improvement

Beyond the technical work, the experience strengthened my ability to adapt engineering and analytical methods to a new international environment.

Disneyland Paris | Process Improvement & Digitalization

Supplier Quality Workflow Automation

Smartsheet · SharePoint · Power Automate · Project Management

Overview

Development and improvement of digital tools designed to streamline Supplier Quality Assurance processes, particularly the management of supplier deviation requests and corrective actions.
The project focused on transforming manual administrative activities into more structured, standardized, and accessible workflows, supporting better coordination between stakeholders.

Challenge

Supplier Quality Assurance activities involve multiple requests, approvals, and follow-up actions requiring coordination between different teams.
Managing these activities through fragmented or manual processes can create administrative complexity, inconsistent information, and limited visibility into request status.
The objective was to develop a more efficient and user-friendly approach to managing quality-related workflows while improving traceability and standardization.

Approach

  • Needs Assessment: Collaborated with stakeholders to understand existing processes, identify challenges, and translate operational requirements into functional specifications.
  • Workflow Design: Developed standardized digital forms and structured tracking systems to organize requests, responsibilities, and follow-up actions.
  • Process Automation: Implemented automated notifications and workflow steps using Smartsheet and Power Automate to reduce repetitive administrative activities.
  • Testing & Optimization: Worked with users to test the solutions, gather feedback, and refine functionality based on operational needs.
  • Documentation & Standardization: Contributed to documenting procedures and improving consistency across quality management activities.

Outcome

The digital solutions contributed to more structured and consistent management of supplier quality requests.
By centralizing information and automating selected administrative steps, the project helped improve process visibility, traceability, and coordination between stakeholders.
It also demonstrated how digital tools can support continuous improvement by making existing processes easier to manage and maintain.

Through technical coursework, practical assignments, and multidisciplinary projects, I have developed the analytical and problem-solving skills needed to understand complex industrial systems, optimize processes, and support engineering decisions.
